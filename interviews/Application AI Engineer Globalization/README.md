# AI Engineer — Technical Interview

Welcome, and thanks for taking the time.

You are joining a team that builds a **cloud-native compliance platform**: it transmits
electronic invoices from ERPs to tax authorities around the world. You don't need any tax
or accounting knowledge for these exercises — they are about **programming logic**.

## Rules

1. You **must** use **Claude Code inside VS Code** to develop.
2. You may use **any programming language** you are comfortable with.
3. **Write your own tests.** How you decide what to test is part of what we evaluate.
4. Be ready to **explain and justify** your solution: why it is correct, how it behaves on
   edge cases, and its time complexity.

## What we look for

- You understand the logic yourself — not only that "it runs".
- You use the AI to move faster, with clear and precise prompts.
- You **read and evaluate** the code the AI produces before accepting it.
◊
## Exercises

- [`exercises/01-latest-invoice-status`](exercises/01-latest-invoice-status) — derive current state from events.
- [`exercises/02-country-checks`](exercises/02-country-checks) — compliance checks per countries.

Do them in order. Aim for correct and clear first; we will discuss trade-offs together.

## Suggested flow per exercise

1. **Read** the exercise README and the sample data.
2. **Solve** it with Claude Code, in the language of your choice.
3. **Write tests** covering the sample plus the edge cases you can think of.
4. **Review** your solution and be ready to walk us through it.

Good luck — have fun with it.
